2 · Identify your products

Prop65 Shield needs to know which product each page displays. Two ways to do this — pick whichever is easier for your setup.

Option A · SKU attribute

Add a data-prop65-sku attribute to your product container:

<div data-prop65-sku="WIDGET-001">
  … your product markup …
</div>

The value must match the Store SKU you set on each product in Products.

Option B · Store URL

No HTML edits needed. Just register each product's page URL in Products and we'll match it automatically.

Works for Shopify, WooCommerce, and any storefront whose product pages have a stable URL.

3 · Test on your site

Open a product page on your live storefront. If everything is set up correctly, the Prop 65 warning banner will appear beneath the product title.

The warning is invisible to everyone except when a warning exists for that product. Nothing appears on unregistered pages.
