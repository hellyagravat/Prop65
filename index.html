08 — Questions

Straight answers, not marketing.

Does this replace attorney review?

No. Every draft is generated for an attorney to review, redline, and sign off on. Nothing is filed or sent to a client without a human lawyer's approval — the system exists to remove drafting hours, not judgment.

How current is the chemical list?

The matching engine syncs against OEHHA's published Proposition 65 list, including additions, delistings, and No Significant Risk Level updates, the same day they're issued.

Can it handle multi-state or multi-retailer variants?

Yes. A single SKU can generate separate warning variants by state, retailer packaging standard, and language, all traced back to the same underlying chemical match.

Is the audit trail actually defensible?

Every match, draft, and approval is timestamped and stored with the citation that generated it. It's built to be produced in discovery or shown to a compliance committee without extra reconstruction work.

What does onboarding look like?

Most firms are matching their first client portfolio within a week — connect a product feed or upload a SKU list, and the system runs its first OEHHA match the same day.
